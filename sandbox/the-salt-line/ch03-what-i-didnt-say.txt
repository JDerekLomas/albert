CHAPTER 3
What I Didn't Say

My mother left in the fall of 1989. It had been building for a long time and I think everyone knew it was coming, even Danny, who was young. She packed over a period of about two weeks and then one morning she was gone, and my father did not explain it to us, and we did not ask him. It was a difficult time for our family and it affected all of us in different ways.

She wrote letters for the first year. Then they came less often. I was angry with her for a long time and then I wasn't, and much later I understood some of the reasons she had for going, though understanding is not the same thing as forgiving and I would not want anyone to think it was.

[This is the chapter I can't write yet. I know what happened and I can't get near it. The morning she left is four hours I remember minute by minute and none of it is here. — R.K.]

The salt was still wet that year when she went.
